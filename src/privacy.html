Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Every page is static HTML with its styles written inline.

What the host records

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub's servers see the address your browser connects from and the page it requests, and GitHub may keep those records for security and operations under its own privacy statement. I have no access to individual visitor records.

Fonts, images, and third parties

Every file on these pages comes from this site. There are no embedded videos, social widgets, web fonts loaded from other companies, or tracking pixels. Links to Upwork and other sites take you to those sites, where their own policies apply.

When you email or call

If you email, call, or text me, I receive your address or number and whatever you choose to send. I use it to reply and, if you hire me, to run the engagement. I don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. Client information, including logins and analytics data you grant me access to, is used only for the work and stays confidential. I remove my access when an engagement ends, or sooner if you ask.

Search engines and AI systems

Search engines and AI crawlers are welcome to read and cite this site; the rules are in robots.txt. That applies to the public pages only. Nothing about visitors is shared with them.
