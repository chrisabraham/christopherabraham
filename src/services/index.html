SEO services

Every service starts from the same place: find out what search engines and AI systems actually receive from your site, compare it with what you meant to publish, and close the gap. Pick the symptom closest to yours, or start with an audit if you aren't sure.

Technical SEO

  • Technical SEO audit: the cause of a traffic loss or a stall, with evidence and a ranked plan.
  • Indexing repair: pages stuck in "Crawled, currently not indexed," "Discovered, currently not indexed," soft 404s, and canonical conflicts.
  • JavaScript and headless SEO: React, Next.js, Vue, Alpine.js, Sanity, Vercel, and other stacks where content depends on scripts.
  • Core Web Vitals and Cloudflare: faster pages measured by real visitors, with caching and CDN rules that don't break crawling.
  • Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and GA4: setup, cleanup, and reports you can trust.
  • Site migrations and 301 redirects: platform moves, domain changes, and redesigns that keep their rankings.

AI search, schema, and content

  • AI SEO, GEO, and AEO: how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and AI Overviews describe you, and how to improve it.
  • Schema markup and entity SEO: JSON-LD that validates, matches the page, and tells machines who you are.
  • On-page SEO: titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, and thin or duplicate pages.

Platforms, local, and ongoing work

  • WordPress SEO: Yoast, Rank Math, themes, plugins, and the performance cleanup old installs need.
  • Ecommerce SEO: Shopify, WooCommerce, and Magento product, category, and faceted pages.
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile: location pages, profiles, categories, and hard cases such as suspensions.
  • Monthly SEO retainer: steady monitoring, fixes, and reporting from a senior consultant.
