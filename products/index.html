Products: fixed scope, fixed price, no proposal required

Most of my work starts with a conversation. This page is the other kind: defined reports with fixed edges and the price printed beside each one. Read exactly what arrives, decide on the spot, and buy through Upwork or begin with a short call. The price printed here is the price charged.

AI Answer Baseline: $500

The Baseline records what the answer engines say about your business today. I run up to twelve questions your buyers actually ask through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews, and I pull Google's organic top ten for the same questions. The report carries a scoreboard for each watched brand: named in the answers or absent, first mentions, engines covered, presence in the web top ten, and best position. The full answer texts are kept as evidence, the citations are ranked in a leaderboard with your own domains flagged, and the takeaways block states only what the recorded answers support.

Fixed edges: one business, up to five watched brands or domains, one market, twelve questions. A larger question set or a second market is a second unit. This report already runs in production and has been delivered for a fireworks retailer with several locations. Order it through my Upwork profile or book a short call to set the question list.

AI Answer Delta: $250 per repeat run

Answers drift, and a single snapshot cannot show movement. The Delta asks the same twelve questions again on a later date and scores the new answers against your Baseline, question by question: who gained, who slipped, which citations changed, and where a competitor took a mention that used to be yours. It is the repeat unit of the Baseline, priced as one, and it requires a Baseline first so the comparison has something solid to stand on.

AI Visibility Audit: $750

The Audit is the Baseline plus my judgment layer. I add an entity and name, address, and phone consistency check across your website, your Google Business Profile, and the main directories. I read the recorded answers for what they get right about you, what they get wrong, and where they hand the customer to a competitor instead. I check the site itself for AI readiness: crawlability, text an engine can quote, and schema presence. The deliverable ends with a written fix list in priority order.

Fixed edges: one business, one market, the audit only. The fixes it names are separate work, quoted after the audit lands. This audit has already been bought at this price, as a pilot engagement, before it ever appeared on this page. The fuller service description lives on AI visibility.

Competitor Visibility Benchmark: $500

The Benchmark scores your brand and up to three named competitors inside the same answer text. Because the engines answer once, every brand is graded on identical evidence: a share of answer table by brand and by engine, first mention share, citation share showing whose domains the engines trust, a head to head map of which brand wins which question, and the competitor pages the engines cite most, listed as the bar to clear. Priced at $500 on its own, or $250 when added to a Baseline. Fixed edges: four brands in total, twelve questions, one market.

Search Visibility Report: $500

This report is built from your own Google Search Console data, sixteen months deep. It opens with headline movement: clicks, impressions, click through rate, and average position, period over period. Then the detail: top queries and top pages with their deltas, a striking distance table of queries sitting in positions five to twenty with real impressions behind them, pages whose titles underperform the position they hold, and index status for up to fifty priority pages.

The sixteen month window is itself the argument. Search Console keeps sixteen months and no more, so every month without a pull, the oldest month of your history falls away for good. You grant read access to the property, and the report is built only from your own data. Fixed edges: one property, one comparison period, fifty inspected pages.

Traffic Truth Report: $500, or $750 paired with the Search Visibility Report

The Traffic Truth Report reads your own GA4 the same way: sessions and conversions by channel group with period deltas, the source and medium table exactly as recorded, landing pages ranked by sessions, engagement, and key events, the device split, a revenue table where ecommerce tracking exists, and data quality flags such as unassigned traffic and missing key events. Bought together with the Search Visibility Report for $750, it adds the cross read: the impressions Google sent set against the sessions GA4 received, with the gaps named. That paired version is the one that settles arguments about where leads actually come from. Fixed edges: one property, 28 day and 90 day windows against comparison periods.

Technical Site Health Snapshot: $500

A crawl based defect ledger for up to 500 URLs, ranked by internal link weight so the pages that matter most sit at the top. It lists status errors, redirect chains and loops with hop counts, missing, duplicate, and over length titles and descriptions page by page, canonical and indexability conflicts, orphan and thin pages, drift between your sitemap and the crawl, an internal link concentration table, and a fix order sequenced by impact against effort. Fixed edges: 500 URLs and the ledger only. The deep audit and the repair work are separate engagements, and this ledger is the honest way to scope them.

Local and GBP Visibility Report: $500

For a business whose buyers decide locally, this report maps how you show up where the decision happens. It builds a map pack presence grid, up to nine location points across five buyer queries, runs a localized AI Answer Baseline for the questions your market asks, names the competitor set exactly as the pack and the answers present it, summarizes your profile state across categories, attributes, photo and review posture, draws review themes from public text, and closes with the fix list in order. Fixed edges: one business, one market, one grid.

Google Business Profile reinstatement: $800, $1,000, or $1,200

When a profile is suspended or disabled, the case work is a product too, in three tiers agreed in writing before anything starts.

  • $800: suspension diagnosis, profile compliance fixes, one properly built appeal with its evidence, and status updates until Google decides.
  • $1,000: everything in the first tier, plus a review of any prior rejection, a service area rebuild where the setup itself is part of the problem, an evidence packet assembled for the case, and a second submission if the case requires one.
  • $1,200: everything in the second tier, plus multi profile and duplicate cleanup and wider footprint remediation, scoped in writing for the case at hand.

Google decides every one of these cases, on its own schedule, and no tier promises an outcome. The price buys the case work, done properly and documented. Related reading: Business Profile verification help and local SEO.
