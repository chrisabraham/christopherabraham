The Baseline records what the answer engines say about your business today. The full answer texts are kept as evidence, the citations are ranked in a leaderboard with your own domains flagged, and the takeaways block states only what the recorded answers support.

Fixed edges: one business, up to five watched brands or domains, one market, twelve questions. A larger question set or a second market is a second unit. ## AI Answer Delta: $250 per repeat run

Answers drift, and a single snapshot cannot show movement. The Delta asks the same twelve questions again on a later date and scores the new answers against your Baseline, question by question: who gained, who slipped, which citations changed, and where a competitor took a mention that used to be yours. It is the repeat unit of the Baseline, priced as one, and it requires a Baseline first so the comparison has something solid to stand on.

## Search Visibility Report: $500

This report is built from your own Google Search Console data, sixteen months deep. It opens with headline movement: clicks, impressions, click through rate, and average position, period over period. Then the detail: top queries and top pages with their deltas, a striking distance table of queries sitting in positions five to twenty with real impressions behind them, pages whose titles underperform the position they hold, and index status for up to fifty priority pages.

The sixteen month window is itself the argument. Search Console keeps sixteen months and no more, so every month without a pull, the oldest month of your history falls away for good. You grant read access to the property, and the report is built only from your own data. Fixed edges: one property, one comparison period, fifty inspected pages.
