## A good first message includes
- The website address and the platform it runs on, if you know it: WordPress, Shopify, Magento, Webflow, a custom React or Next.js build, or something else.
- The symptom in your own words: traffic down, pages not indexed, slow pages, a migration coming, a suspended profile, odd AI answers.
- The date it started, and anything that changed around then, such as a redesign, plugin update, new host, or new developer.
- A screenshot from Search Console or analytics if one tells the story.
- A rough budget and deadline.
