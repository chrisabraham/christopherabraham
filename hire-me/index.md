Every engagement follows the same arc: a defined question, a paid audit that answers it, a ranked plan, the fixes, and a verification pass once the fixes are live. Here's how each step works and what I need from you.

## Two ways to hire me
- **On Upwork.** Most of my clients hire me through [my Upwork profile](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/chrisjabraham). Engagements there run as fixed price contracts with milestones, and Upwork handles the contract, invoicing, and payment protection for both sides.

## The process
1. **A short conversation.** You tell me the symptom; I ask what changed, when, and who has access to what. A few emails or a [30 minute call](https://calendly.com/chrisabraham/30) is usually enough.
2. **A written scope.** The questions the audit will answer, what you'll receive, the access I'll need, the timeline, and the price.
3. **The audit.** Crawls, Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools data, rendered HTML, server responses, and whatever else the question calls for. You receive a findings report with the cause, the evidence, and a ranked list of fixes.
4. **The fixes.** I make the changes I have access to, such as metadata, redirects, schema, plugin settings, and Cloudflare rules. For everything that needs a developer, I write tickets with steps and acceptance criteria.
5. **Verification.** Once changes ship, I check the live site, request recrawls where they help, and watch the reports until the trend is clear.

## What I need from you
- Access to Google Search Console and, if you have it, Bing Webmaster Tools. Full user access is ideal; I'll explain why when it matters.
- Read access to analytics, usually GA4.
- A content management login with a role that fits the work, or a staging copy.
- An introduction to whoever deploys code, if that isn't you.
- A named person who answers questions and approves changes.

## Where the work lives

I keep every engagement in writing: Upwork messages, email, or your project tool, whether that's Basecamp, Asana, Jira, or a shared Google Drive folder. Calls are welcome for kickoff and walkthroughs, and each call gets a written follow-up so decisions survive. A written record makes the work easier to review, easier to hand to a developer, and easier to pick up six months later.

## Pricing

I quote every engagement as a fixed fee. Audits are scoped and priced before they start, so you know the cost of an answer before you commit to it. Follow-on work is quoted per milestone on the same basis, and retainers are a fixed monthly fee for an agreed scope, with a report every month. The price is settled in conversation before the work begins, never by a clock.

## What I sell, and what nobody can

I sell expertise, diagnosis, and hands-on work. Rankings, indexing decisions, AI answers, and Google Business Profile reviews belong to the platforms, and each one decides on its own schedule. So I commit to the work and the evidence, and I report honestly on what the platforms do in response.
