The answer on top is the new front door.

Google's AI Overviews now appear for 88% of commercial searches, nearly double what they covered a year ago. Your customers meet an AI's version of your business before they ever reach your website. I make sure that answer names you, describes you correctly, and cites you—and then I prove it, monthly, in writing.

The problem, plainly

Search used to be a list of links. Now it's an answer, written by a machine, assembled from whatever the machine could find, verify, and trust about you. If your name, address, services, and facts are scattered or inconsistent, the answer names your competitor instead. Review searches—"best," "near me," "which one should I pick"—moved furthest of all. That is the exact moment a customer chooses.

You don't get to opt out of being summarized. You only get to choose whether the summary is accurate.

What I do

1. AI Visibility Audit—$750 flat

I check where your business appears, and where it doesn't, across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, for the searches that actually bring you money. I record what the AI answers say about you: right, wrong, or your competitor's name. I check your entity and name-address-phone consistency across your website, your Google Business Profile, and the directories. You get a written report with the fix list in priority order.

No retainer. No vague deliverable. A document you can hand to anyone.

2. The Claim Sprint—$1,500 flat

The audit tells you what's broken. The sprint fixes the layer that fixes it:

  • Technical foundation: full crawl, Search Console review, titles and metas, broken links and redirects
  • The claim layer: entity schema with sameAs links, an llms.txt file, a canonical "what is this business" page, and FAQ schema, so the machines can quote you instead of guessing at you

One honest note: I am an SEO, not a web developer. Anything that belongs in a developer's hands comes back to you as a precise, written fix list your developer can execute without a meeting.

3. AI Presence, Measured—$500/month

AI answers drift. Competitors publish, directories change, and the answer that named you in March may name someone else in June. Every month I re-run your money-query set across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, and you get a written receipt: what changed, what the answers say now, and what gets fixed next.

Proof this is already working

  • A multi-location fireworks retailer has AI-answer presence measurement built into its engagement: a baseline first, then measured monthly across Google Search, Maps, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.
  • A therapy practice received the full audit treatment: entity and name-address-phone consistency checked against everything the AI systems read.
  • A media company's engagement included the claim layer itself: entity schema, llms.txt, a canonical explainer page, and an AI-presence baseline.
  • The audit has already sold at this price as a pilot for a communications firm. The price is the price because it has been paid.
