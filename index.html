Senior SEO help, from the person who does the work

I'm Christopher Abraham, an independent SEO consultant. When traffic drops, pages vanish from Google, or AI assistants describe your business wrong, I find the cause, write the fix, and make sure it lands. You work with me directly, from the first audit to the last verification crawl.

This year's work looks like this: GEO and AEO audits that test what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and Google's AI Overviews say about a business; rendering forensics on React, Next.js, and Hyvä builds; SEO changes merged through GitHub on headless stacks; and AI-assisted analysis of a site's code before a migration. The fundamentals carry across every engine. Pages that load fast, render cleanly, say plainly what they are about, and carry accurate structured data get crawled, indexed, ranked, and quoted.

I know those fundamentals cold because I've been practicing them a long time: I built my first websites in 1994 and started doing search engine optimization in 1998, back when the job meant hand-editing meta tags for AltaVista. Every shift since then, from links to mobile to AI answers, has been one more thing to learn, and learning it is my favorite part of the job.

Problems I solve

  • Traffic dropped and nobody knows why. A technical SEO audit that ends with a cause, evidence, and a ranked list of fixes.
  • Google won't index the pages. Indexing repair for "Crawled, currently not indexed," soft 404s, stray canonicals, and orphaned pages.
  • The site runs on React and nothing ranks. JavaScript SEO for client-rendered, headless, and framework sites.
  • ChatGPT has never heard of the business. AI SEO, GEO, and AEO: being understood and cited by answer engines.
  • A platform move is coming next month. Migration planning and 301 redirect maps that carry your rankings across.
  • Pages are slow. Core Web Vitals and Cloudflare work measured in field data.
  • Customers can't find the business on the map. Local SEO and Google Business Profile alignment.

The complete list, including schema markup, WordPress SEO, ecommerce SEO, and monthly retainers, is on the services page.

Why clients pick me

  • Seniority without the agency layer. The person who scopes the work is the person who crawls the site, reads the logs, and writes the developer tickets.
  • I read code. Templates, rendered DOM, build settings, and pull requests are part of the job. Developers get specifications with acceptance criteria, and I check what ships.
  • Evidence first. When two problems arrive at once, I keep them on separate tracks until the data connects them.
  • Plain English. Every engagement ends with a technical document for engineers and a short summary for whoever signs the checks.
